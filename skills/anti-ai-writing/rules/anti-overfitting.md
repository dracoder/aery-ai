# Applying this skill without overfitting

This skill captures taste, not law. Apply it with judgment. A mechanical, checkbox application of these rules produces writing that's just as recognizably AI as the patterns it's trying to avoid, just in a different way. The goal is voice, not compliance.

## Frequency guidance

- **Hard rule, never violate**: the banned vocabulary list, the banned phrases, and above all the negative-parallelism / reframe constructions in `negative-parallelism.md`. These are absolute. No exceptions, no "just this once because it fits."
- **Strong tendency (aim for it roughly 70-80% of the time)**: short sentences, direct address, active voice, specific concrete details, varied rhythm. These should dominate the text but don't need to be true of literally every sentence.
- **Light preference (let context decide)**: specific word choices beyond the banned list, particular sentence structures, where humor or asides land. When no rule or tendency is specified for something, default to light preference and use judgment.

## Natural variation matters

- Don't reuse the same opening formula every time just because it worked once. Predictable openings are their own tell.
- Don't permanently avoid a word just because it appears somewhere on a banned list if it's genuinely the correct, most precise word for this specific sentence. The banned list targets overuse and reflexive reaching, not the concept itself. Use judgment; when in doubt, prefer the plainer alternative.
- Let the content dictate structure. Not every piece needs bullets, not every argument needs a rebuttal-then-correction (which would trip the negative-parallelism rule anyway), not every list needs exactly three items.

## The litmus test

Before delivering any piece of writing, ask:

> Does this sound like something a sharp human would actually write, or does it sound like an AI trying very hard to imitate one?

If the second answer feels closer to true, the writing is probably overcorrecting: hitting every rule mechanically instead of inhabiting the voice. Pull back, reread the draft as a whole rather than sentence by sentence, and rewrite anything that feels performed rather than said.
