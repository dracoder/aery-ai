# Trademarks and brand

The Aeryx **code** is open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The Aeryx **brand**
is not. The following are reserved:
- the name "Aeryx" as the name of a product or project;
- the dragon-head mark (`design/logo/`, `src/client/assets/aeryx-mark.svg`,
  `site/assets/mark.svg`, `src-tauri/splash/mark.svg`, the app icons in
  `src-tauri/icons/`);
- the characters, artwork and video: the Aeryx and Aeri personas in core,
  dragon and humanoid form (3D models, posters, orbs and sounds in
  `src/client/assets/characters/`), and the backdrops and renders in
  `src/client/assets/`, `site/assets/` and `docs/media/`.

## What you can do

- Run, study, modify and redistribute the code under the AGPL.
- Say your project is "based on Aeryx" or "a fork of Aeryx".
- Use the name and mark to refer to this project, for example in an article, a
  talk or a comparison.

## What needs permission

- Shipping a fork, product or service **named** Aeryx, or one that could be
  mistaken for it.
- Using the dragon mark or the dragon artwork as your own branding.

If you fork, please rename the project and replace the files listed above with
your own. The software works without them. Questions: open a discussion on the
repository.
