Privacy

This website

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you join the release list, your email address is sent through FormSubmit to the project maintainer. It is used for one purpose: to tell you when a new release ships. It is not sold or shared. To be removed, reply to any email from the list.

The Aeryx software

  • Aeryx runs on your computer. It has no server of its own, and it sends no telemetry and no crash reports.
  • What you ask goes to the model provider you choose: a local Ollama model (your asks stay on your machine), Anthropic, or OpenRouter. That provider's privacy terms apply to those requests.
  • The news panel fetches public RSS feeds. Research and web tools fetch the pages they need.
  • Spoken replies use Microsoft Edge's online text-to-speech, so the text being spoken is sent to Microsoft. Turn it off with "ttsEnabled": false.
  • Your conversations, memory, audit log and settings stay on your disk. Model keys are kept out of aeryx.config.json: in the macOS Keychain, encrypted with Windows DPAPI, or on Linux in a 0600 file under secrets/.
